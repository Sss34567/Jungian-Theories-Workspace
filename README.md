# Jungian Theories in the Workspace (Data Analytics Project)

## Overview
Due to the importance of mental health, its impact on development, and productivity in society—and driven by my interest in psychology and related theories—I designed my first data analytics project to blend the technical and psychological domains.

**Project title:** Jungian Theories in the Workspace  
**Research question:** *How do Jung’s theories interpret employee data?*

## Dataset
The dataset includes the following employee indicators:
- Working hours
- Sleep hours
- Stress levels
- Burnout indicators

## Jungian Mapping (Conceptual Framework)

### 1) Persona (القناع)
**Interpretation:** The *persona* appears in **long working hours** performed to satisfy societal expectations and workplace norms.

### 2) Shadow (الظل)
**Interpretation:** The *shadow* emerges clearly through **burnout indicators**—the repressed side that surfaces when we ignore our need for rest and recovery.

## Tools & Tech Stack
- **SQL:** Cleaning and preprocessing raw employee data.
- **Power BI:** Building an interactive dashboard linking psychological variables with work performance.

## Key Recommendations
- Convert eligible departments to a **remote-work** model.
- **Redistribute work hours** across employees instead of a fixed 8 hours per employee.
- Provide a **comfortable, supportive work environment**.
- Link promotions to **creativity and innovation**.
- Offer an annual **voucher for a mental health clinic** for employees.

## Deliverables
- SQL cleaning scripts/queries (data preprocessing)
- Power BI interactive dashboard (analysis + visualization)
